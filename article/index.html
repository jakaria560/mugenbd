Top 25 Anime You Should Watch

Looking for your next favorite anime? From unforgettable action adventures to emotional films and clever comedies, here are 25 anime series and movies worth adding to your watchlist.

Anime offers something for almost every kind of viewer. Some stories deliver spectacular battles and powerful heroes, while others explore friendship, personal growth, romance and the meaning of life. This list brings together popular classics and newer favorites to help you decide what to watch next.

25 Anime Recommendations

  1. Naruto Naruto Uzumaki dreams of becoming the Hokage and earning the respect of his village. His journey is filled with friendship, rivalry, emotional moments and unforgettable ninja battles.
  2. One Piece Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew travel across dangerous seas in search of the legendary One Piece treasure. The series combines adventure, comedy, emotional storytelling and creative battles.
  3. Attack on Titan Humanity fights for survival behind enormous walls while terrifying Titans threaten its existence. As the mystery unfolds, the story explores freedom, sacrifice and the cost of war.
  4. Death Note Light Yagami discovers a mysterious notebook that can kill anyone whose name is written in it. His attempt to reshape the world leads to a tense battle of intelligence with detective L.
  5. Demon Slayer Tanjiro Kamado becomes a demon slayer after a tragedy strikes his family. He sets out to protect others and find a way to turn his sister Nezuko back into a human.
  6. Jujutsu Kaisen Yuji Itadori enters the dangerous world of curses after swallowing a powerful cursed object. He trains alongside sorcerers while facing terrifying enemies and difficult choices.
  7. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood Brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric search for the Philosopher's Stone after a failed alchemy experiment costs them dearly. Their journey combines action, mystery and powerful themes of family and sacrifice.
  8. Hunter x Hunter Gon Freecss becomes a Hunter to find his missing father. Along the way, he makes close friends, faces dangerous challenges and discovers a world full of extraordinary abilities.
  9. My Hero Academia In a world where most people have special powers, Izuku Midoriya begins as a powerless boy and trains to become a top hero.
  10. One Punch Man Saitama can defeat any enemy with a single punch, and his search for a worthy opponent makes for a funny and exciting parody of superhero stories.
  11. Code Geass An exiled prince gains a mysterious power and leads a rebellion against a powerful empire, using strategy as his main weapon.
  12. Vinland Saga A young Viking warrior searches for meaning and revenge, and slowly discovers what it truly means to live without violence.
  13. Mob Psycho 100 A shy middle school boy with huge psychic power tries to live a normal life while learning to manage his emotions.
  14. Your Name Two teenagers who have never met begin swapping bodies, and a touching romance unfolds across time and distance.
  15. A Silent Voice A former bully tries to make amends with a deaf classmate he once hurt, in a gentle story about guilt, forgiveness and friendship.
  16. Spy x Family A spy, an assassin and a telepathic girl pretend to be an ordinary family, and each secretly hides who they really are.
  17. Haikyu!! A short but determined boy joins a high school volleyball team and shows that passion and teamwork can beat natural talent.
  18. Violet Evergarden A former soldier writes letters for other people and slowly learns to understand human feelings in this beautifully animated drama.
  19. Dragon Ball Z Goku and his friends defend Earth against powerful enemies in one of the classic anime that shaped the entire genre.
  20. Bleach Ichigo Kurosaki gains the powers of a Soul Reaper and protects the living from evil spirits while exploring the world of the dead.
  21. Chainsaw Man Denji, a poor young man in heavy debt, merges with a chainsaw devil and joins a group that hunts dangerous devils.
  22. Frieren: Beyond Journey's End After the hero's party defeats the Demon King, an elf mage looks back on her long life and learns to value the short time humans have.
  23. Neon Genesis Evangelion Teenage pilots control giant machines to stop mysterious beings, in a deep and psychological story that changed anime forever.
  24. Tokyo Ghoul College student Ken Kaneki finds himself caught between the human world and the world of flesh-eating ghouls after a life-changing encounter.
  25. Steins;Gate A group of friends discovers a way to send messages into the past. Their experiments lead to unexpected consequences and a gripping story about time, choices and sacrifice.

Every viewer has different tastes, so treat this list as a starting point instead of a strict ranking. Start with one or two titles that sound interesting, and you will probably find your next favorite along the way.
