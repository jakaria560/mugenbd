Top 25 Anime You Should Watch

A fresh guide to 25 anime series and films worth watching, from long-running adventures to short emotional stories.

  1. Naruto Naruto Uzumaki dreams of becoming the strongest ninja and earning the respect of his village.
  2. One Piece Monkey D. Luffy and his crew travel across dangerous seas in search of the legendary One Piece treasure.
  3. Attack on Titan Humanity fights for survival behind enormous walls while terrifying Titans threaten the remaining cities.
  4. Death Note A student discovers a supernatural notebook that can kill anyone whose name is written in it.
  5. Demon Slayer Tanjiro Kamado becomes a demon slayer after a tragedy strikes his family and sets out to save his sister.
  6. Jujutsu Kaisen Yuji Itadori enters the dangerous world of curses after swallowing a powerful cursed object.
  7. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood Two brothers search for the Philosopher's Stone after an alchemy experiment goes terribly wrong.
  8. Hunter x Hunter Gon Freecss becomes a Hunter and travels through a dangerous world to find his father.
  9. My Hero Academia In a world where most people have special powers, Izuku Midoriya begins as a powerless boy and trains to become a top hero.
  10. One Punch Man Saitama can defeat any enemy with a single punch, and his search for a worthy opponent makes for a funny and exciting parody of superhero stories.
  11. Code Geass An exiled prince gains a mysterious power and leads a rebellion against a powerful empire, using strategy as his main weapon.
  12. Vinland Saga A young Viking warrior searches for meaning and revenge, and slowly discovers what it truly means to live without violence.
  13. Mob Psycho 100 A shy middle school boy with huge psychic power tries to live a normal life while learning to manage his emotions.
  14. Your Name Two teenagers who have never met begin swapping bodies, and a touching romance unfolds across time and distance.
  15. A Silent Voice A former bully tries to make amends with a deaf classmate he once hurt, in a gentle story about guilt, forgiveness and friendship.
  16. Spy x Family A spy, an assassin and a telepathic girl pretend to be an ordinary family, and each secretly hides who they really are.
  17. Haikyu!! A short but determined boy joins a high school volleyball team and shows that passion and teamwork can beat natural talent.
  18. Violet Evergarden A former soldier writes letters for other people and slowly learns to understand human feelings in this beautifully animated drama.
  19. Dragon Ball Z Goku and his friends defend Earth against powerful enemies in one of the classic anime that shaped the entire genre.
  20. Bleach Ichigo Kurosaki gains the powers of a Soul Reaper and protects the living from evil spirits while exploring the world of the dead.
  21. Chainsaw Man Denji, a poor young man in heavy debt, merges with a chainsaw devil and joins a group that hunts dangerous devils.
  22. Frieren: Beyond Journey's End After the hero's party defeats the Demon King, an elf mage looks back on her long life and learns to value the short time humans have.
  23. Neon Genesis Evangelion Teenage pilots control giant machines to stop mysterious beings, in a deep and psychological story that changed anime forever.

Every viewer has different tastes, so treat this list as a starting point instead of a strict ranking. Start with one or two titles that sound interesting, and you will probably find your next favorite along the way.

Anime offers countless stories, characters and adventures for every type of viewer.

Whether you enjoy action, fantasy, comedy or emotional stories, there is always something new to discover.
